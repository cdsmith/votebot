# Privacy Policy

*Last updated: October 20, 2026*

Electable stores only what it needs to run elections, and deletes anything about voters when an election ends. This policy covers the public instance. Anyone can run their own copy from the source code, and this policy does not apply to those.

## What is collected

**Discord user IDs.** The bot keeps the numeric Discord user IDs of people who vote in, create, or manage an open election. It does not collect names, usernames, email addresses, IP addresses, passwords, or anything else that identifies you.

**Ballots.** While an election is open, the bot stores each voter's ballot along with their user ID. This is what enforces one vote per person and lets you change your vote.

**Election content.** Titles, descriptions, candidates, and settings, written by whoever sets up the election.

**Aggregate results.** Totals and tallies computed from the ballots. These contain no user IDs and no individual ballots.

## Who can see it

**Individual ballots:** no one looks at who voted or how. That includes the people running the election, server admins, and the maintainer.

**Aggregate results:** the people running an election may see them while it is open, and everyone in the channel may see them once it ends. In an election with very few voters, it may be possible to infer individual votes from the totals.

**Election content, and who is running an election:** everyone in the channel, since elections are posted there.

Data is used only to run elections. There is no analytics, advertising, or profiling, and data is not sold or shared with anyone for their own use.

The bot runs on a single cloud-hosted server, so the hosting provider has the same operational access to the data that any host has. The bot also runs on Discord, and Discord sees everything the bot does. Discord's handling of your data is covered by [Discord's Privacy Policy](https://discord.com/privacy).

## Retention

**User IDs and ballots** are deleted when their election ends or is deleted. There is no fixed time limit while an election is open.

**Aggregate results** are kept for at most 30 days after the election ends.

**Messages the bot posts** in your channels are under your server's control, like any other message.

## Your choices

- **Change your vote** any time before the election ends.
- **Delete an election:** deleting an election, or its message in the channel, deletes every ballot in it.
- **Stop all data collection:** remove the bot from your server. Its elections in your server, and every ballot in them, are deleted when it is removed.
